About Us

Sip & Savour Cafe is a local cafe based in Rosebank, Johannesburg. We provide fresh food, coffee, desserts and a comfortable space for customers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve good quality food and drinks in a friendly environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a well-known local cafe that customers enjoy visiting.

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Friendly service
  • Community
  • Customer satisfaction

Target Customers

Our target customers include students, young professionals, families, remote workers and tourists.
