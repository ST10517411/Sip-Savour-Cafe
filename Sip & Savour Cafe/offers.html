Weekly Promotions

We have different specials available throughout the week. Customers can enjoy selected meals and drinks at a reduced price.

  • Monday: Buy any breakfast meal and get a regular coffee for only R25.
  • Wednesday: Get 10% off any lunch meal when you dine in.
  • Friday: Enjoy a slice of cake and a regular coffee for R75.
  • Weekend Special: Enjoy our breakfast and fresh juice combination for R105.

Weekly promotions may change, so customers are encouraged to check our website or visit the cafe for the latest deals.

Seasonal Desserts

Our dessert menu changes during different seasons so that customers can enjoy new flavours throughout the year.

  • Summer: Fresh fruit cheesecake and homemade lemon tart.
  • Autumn: Warm apple crumble served with vanilla ice cream.
  • Winter: Chocolate brownie served warm with hot chocolate sauce.
  • Spring: Fresh berry muffins and strawberry cheesecake.

Seasonal desserts are available while stocks last. Customers can ask our staff about the dessert of the day.

Upcoming Events

Sip and Savour Cafe plans to host small events that give customers an opportunity to enjoy good food, drinks and entertainment in a relaxed environment.

  • Friday Live Music: Enjoy live acoustic music while having dinner and drinks.
  • Saturday Coffee Morning: Join us for coffee, pastries and a relaxed start to the weekend.
  • Holiday Specials: During public holidays and special occasions, we may offer special meals and discounted drinks.
  • Community Events: We may host small community gatherings and social events at the cafe.

Event dates and times will be announced in advance. Customers can contact the cafe for more information or to reserve a table for an event.
